A simple routine

Three steps from idea to done

You do not need a complicated system. Start with the person, set a comfortable budget, and save a few possibilities.

1

Think about the person

Note what they enjoy, what they use every day, or something they have mentioned wanting. A small clue is a better starting point than a random bestseller.

2

Choose a budget

Decide what feels right before you shop. For group occasions, split the total across the people on your list so one gift does not use the whole budget.

3

Save a few good ideas

Keep links and notes together, compare your options, then mark the gift you choose. You can stop reopening dozens of tabs and wondering what you planned.

About the app

Keep the whole plan in one place

Use GiftList & WishList Maker to keep event lists, recipient budgets, and gift ideas together on your Android device.

1

Create an occasion

Start a list for a birthday, holiday, or another event. Add the people you are shopping for and set a budget that works for you.

2

Save your ideas

Collect possible gifts and notes for each person. You can share a product link into the app to keep useful details with your list.

3

Choose and track

Compare the options you saved, decide what to buy, and keep track of the gifts you have planned. Purchases are completed with the retailer.
