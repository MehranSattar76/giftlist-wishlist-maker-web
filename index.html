Universal Link Scraping

Simple Workflow

How GiftList & WishList Maker Works

Three intuitive steps to take you from brainstorm to wrapped gifts without exceeding your budget.

1

Setup Events & Budget

Create an event such as "Family Christmas" or "Birthday Bash". Add your recipients and assign individual spending targets.

2

Browse & Collect Candidates

Surf online stores. Whenever you spot a potential gift on Amazon, eBay, or other web stores, share the link into the app to grab its image, title, and current price.

3

Compare & Purchase

Review candidate items side-by-side. Mark the winning choice as "Gifts to Buy", archive extra ideas, and tap directly through to the merchant to order.
